Vision

Build a reliable, fast, and scalable farm-to-door marketplace that supplies fruits, vegetables, and meat to households (≤60‑minute delivery) and bulk buyers (supermarkets, restaurants, vendors). The platform must also let vendors and farmers advertise and sell their own products.

Core User Roles

Household Shopper – places on‑demand orders; expects real‑time tracking and delivery ≤60 minutes.

Business Buyer (B2B) – supermarkets, restaurants, vendors; places scheduled or recurring bulk orders with invoicing and delivery windows.

Vendor/Farmer – lists products, manages stock, prices, ads, and fulfillment preferences (consign to your hubs or self‑fulfill for local pickup/delivery).

Operations – dispatchers, pickers, drivers, quality control, finance.

Admin – compliance, catalog curation, dispute resolution, platform settings.

Key Value Propositions

Speed: 60‑minute SLA for household orders inside geo‑fenced service areas.

Reliability: Live inventory, cold‑chain handling, proactive substitution rules, ETA updates.

Scale: Scheduled bulk logistics, pallet/crate units, purchase orders, EDI/CSV imports.

Marketplace: Vendors + farmers can advertise and sell; you earn commissions + ad revenue.

Feature Set (MVP → v1.0)
A) Catalog & Inventory

Unified catalog for fruits, vegetables, meat with variants (grade, cut, weight band, organic).

Lot‑level tracking (batch, harvest/pack date, expiry, temperature band).

Real‑time stock syncing from hubs and vendor stores.

Substitution policies (auto/ask/never) and equivalents mapping.

B) Ordering Flows

Households (On‑Demand):

Service area check → inventory from nearest hub(s) → cart → time promise (≤60 min) → pay → live tracking.

Smart pack sizes (e.g., 1kg, 2kg; meat by cut & weight).

Add‑ons at checkout (sauces, herbs, sides).

B2B (Bulk/Scheduled):

Request‑for‑Quote (RFQ) or contract pricing.

Purchase Orders (POs): pallets/crates/boxes; delivery windows; recurring schedules.

Multi‑drop routes, dock scheduling, ASN/GRN documents; optional credit terms.

Vendor/Farmer Sales:

Self‑serve storefronts with tiered pricing (retail vs wholesale).

Consign‑to‑hub or direct‑fulfill options.

Ad placements (search, category, homepage tiles) with budget & targeting.

C) Fulfillment & Logistics

Micro‑fulfillment hubs (cold‑chain capable) + dark‑store picking for speed.

Picker app: wave/batch picking, weight capture, photo QC, substitution prompts.

Dispatch: auto‑assignment, batching, route optimization, driver app with turn‑by‑turn and proof‑of‑delivery.

Temperature compliance: insulated totes, gel packs; hub sensors; chain‑of‑custody logs.

D) Tracking & Notifications

Real‑time map, ETA updates, order status events (Accepted → Picking → Picked → Out‑for‑delivery → Arrived → Delivered).

SMS/WhatsApp/push notifications; driver contact masking; delivery notes & photos.

E) Payments & Finance

Cards, mobile wallets, EFT; split payments to vendors; escrow until delivery.

B2B invoicing, credit limits, statements, partial fulfillment handling.

Refunds/adjustments (weight variances), tips, promos, and gift credits.

F) Reliability & Quality

SLA engine with penalties & auto‑refund tiers.

Shelf‑life rules: block items close to expiry for far ETAs.

Forecasting to prevent stock‑outs; demand vs supply heatmaps.

Service‑Level Architecture

Frontends

Web (Next.js) for shoppers and B2B buyers.

Mobile apps (React Native) for shoppers & drivers.

Vendor Portal (web) and Operations Console (internal).

Backends

API (Node.js/TypeScript + NestJS or FastAPI): GraphQL for read models, REST for commands.

Services: Catalog, Inventory, Orders, Fulfillment, Dispatch, Payments, Vendors, Ads, Notifications, Analytics.

Workers: ETA calculation, route optimization, SLA monitoring, invoicing, forecasting.

Data

PostgreSQL (OLTP) with row‑level security for vendors.

Redis for sessions, rate limits, queues, and real‑time location.

Object storage for images/PODs.

Warehouse (BigQuery/Redshift) + dbt for analytics.

Integrations

Maps & distance matrix, SMS/WhatsApp, payment gateway, accounting (Xero/QuickBooks), optional EDI for large supermarkets.

Scalability & Reliability

Auto‑scaling containers, circuit breakers, idempotent command handlers, retries with backoff.

Observability: metrics (SLIs/SLOs), tracing, alerting; audit logs.
